# Timeline of the Negotiation

A chronological record of the billing arrangement for the 2025 renovation works
and every subsequent change requested by SmartSpace. Dates should be filled in
from calendars, chat logs, and the Google Sheet revision history where
available.

## Phase 0 — Before the renovation

- Autolifetech completed previous projects for SmartSpace, invoiced as a
  standard vendor, and was paid normally.
- Relationship context: Clement (Autolifetech) and Yvonne (SmartSpace) are
  long-time friends.

## Phase 1 — Agreed basis: discounted hourly rate

- During the 2025 renovation, Clement agreed to bill by **hourly rate,
  pro-rated similarly to employment**, explicitly to reduce SmartSpace's total
  cost. This was a concession from standard vendor pricing, offered as a
  favour.

## Meeting 1 — Hourly costing presented → change to itemised billing

- Clement presented a Google Sheet with **estimated hours and costing**.
- Yvonne's response: do **not** bill by hours. Instead, take the discounted
  hourly-derived prices and repackage them as **itemised billing**, in the
  same style as vendors Zuh / Nabil.
- Stated rationale: Aiveen (accountant) would not accept an hourly rate.
- Promise made: payment would be made **promptly** once billed this way.

## Meeting 2 — Itemised billing presented → change to big categories

- Clement presented the itemised bill. It was long and extensive (accurately
  reflecting the technical scope).
- Yvonne's response: change again — consolidate into **big categories**
  (lights, aircon, ventilation, ceiling, etc.) and "tell the story" behind
  each: planning, sourcing vendors, meetings, weekend work.
- Presumed rationale: a long technical list would be impossible for Aiveen to
  check line by line.

## Meeting 3 — Big categories presented → change to cost-plus

- Clement presented the big-category billing with pricing.
- Yvonne appeared unhappy and changed direction again: she asked Clement to
  send **Autolifetech's opex** (internal operating costs).
- The arrangement shifted to a **cost-plus model** — a further discount below
  the already-discounted hourly basis.
- Promise made: prompt payment after the details were sent.

## Meeting 4 — Opex disclosed → no agreement

- Autolifetech's opex was sent and presented.
- Yvonne was **not agreeable** even to cost-plus. She said she needed to
  discuss with her team. No payment.

## Meeting 5 — Monthly-fee contract proposed (current position)

After discussing with her team, Yvonne proposed a **monthly fee model**:

- A contract under which Autolifetech is paid **X per month for the last 6
  months of 2025**, and a **lower Y per month for the first 6 months of 2026**.
- Yvonne stated this structure is "**just for the accountant to see**" — the
  real purpose is to split the amount into next year's budget.
- Verbal promises attached:
  - If Clement signs, the first 6 months (2025 portion) will be paid
    **immediately**.
  - The second tranche — which is actually payment for **2025 works** — will
    "definitely" be paid **before Chinese New Year** (i.e. before/around
    Feb 2026).
- Financial effect: the total under this model is below Autolifetech's cost.
  The arrangement has moved from **cost-plus to a loss model**.

## Phase 2 — After the monthly-fee contract (Jan–Jul 2026)

Documented in detail, with quotes, in [04-evidence-log.md](04-evidence-log.md).
The contract was signed; the arrangement now runs as a monthly salary tied to
7 days/month of service at SmartSpace.

- **15 Jan 2026** — Clement states in writing he is not confident of payment
  even covering basic cost. Yvonne: "I'm making $$ as fast as we can to pay,
  Clement" — written acknowledgment that money is owed.
- **3 Feb 2026** — Clement tallies Yeemax's invoices for SmartSpace (free
  verification work, identifying savings). Yvonne, in writing: "we will close
  with Yeemax first then move on to the other payments. **All before CNY.**"
- **17 Feb 2026** — Chinese New Year. No payment.
- **23 Feb – 6 Mar 2026** — Clement chases the "alt payment" repeatedly.
  Responses: "Missed this msg yest! Ok will check", travel small-talk, new
  task requests. On 6 Mar Yvonne raises: continuation of the 7-days/month
  salary arrangement, a possible "total payout of the Jan-Jun monies, if not
  partial" tied to who fulfils future service, and attempted deductions
  (20% Yeemax commissions; "$500 worth of equipment that might have been
  taken"). Clement rejects deductions; Yvonne accepts ("OK", "On it!").
- **10–11 Mar 2026** — New invoice re-format requested. Aiveen returns from
  medical leave and conditions payment on Clement paying an invoice from
  Yvonne's side first (the entangled corp-sec/services relationship).
- **24 Mar 2026** — Payment said to be "inside" the monthly payroll file with
  Clement's salary — a drip via payroll instead of the promised transfer.
- **10 Jul 2026** — Clement confirms from his accounts: "last year Reno
  payment not done… came out at a loss. Make the transfer before any other
  interviews of meeting." Yvonne drops "interviews" but still requires a
  Monday meeting; payment remains unmade. Clement: "What happened to all
  these?"

## Constant conditions across all meetings

- Meetings with Yvonne were hard to schedule; every meeting was rushed and the
  agenda was never fully completed.
- At each stage, payment was promised contingent on the *next* re-formatting of
  the bill. No payment has been made under any format.

## Summary of billing-model drift

| Stage | Model | Direction of value |
|---|---|---|
| Original norm | Standard vendor pricing | Baseline |
| Agreed for reno | Discounted hourly (pro-rated, employment-like) | Discount #1 |
| Meeting 1 | Same total, repackaged as itemised billing | Format change |
| Meeting 2 | Same total, repackaged as big categories + narrative | Format change |
| Meeting 3–4 | Cost-plus (after opex disclosure) | Discount #2 |
| Meeting 5 | Monthly fee split across 2025/2026 | Below cost — **loss** |
