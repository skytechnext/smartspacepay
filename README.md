# Autolifetech ↔ SmartSpace — 2025 Renovation Payment Case File

This repository documents the ongoing payment negotiation between **Autolifetech**
(vendor, owned by Clement) and **SmartSpace** (client, owned by Yvonne) for
renovation works carried out in 2025.

## Background

- Autolifetech has completed works for SmartSpace before, billed and paid as a
  normal vendor.
- Clement and Yvonne are long-time friends.
- For the 2025 renovation, Clement agreed to help SmartSpace reduce cost by
  billing at an hourly rate, pro-rated similarly to employment, instead of
  standard vendor pricing.
- The works have been **completed and delivered**. Payment remains outstanding.
- Over a series of rushed meetings, the billing basis has been changed five
  times at SmartSpace's request, each change lowering Autolifetech's recovery,
  and payment has not been made under any of them.
